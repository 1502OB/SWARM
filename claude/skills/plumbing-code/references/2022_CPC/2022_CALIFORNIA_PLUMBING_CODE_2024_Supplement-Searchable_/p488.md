Testing and Inspection Requirements
Media
The piping of the building sewer shall be tested with
water. The Authority Having Jurisdiction shall be
permitted to require the removal of cleanouts, etc., to
ascertain whether the pressure has reached all parts
of the system.
Water Test
The system shall be tested by plugging the end of
the building sewer at its points of connection to the
public sewer or private sewage disposal system and
completely filling the building sewer with water
from the lowest to the highest point thereof.
Inspections
The completed piping shall be internally inspected
by camera unless waived by the Administrative
Authority.
Watertight and Gastight
Cleanouts shall be designed to be watertight and
gastight.
