Scope
General
This standard shall govern the trenchless installation
of polyethylene (PE) pipe for use in sanitary and
storm sewers. The installed pipe shall comply with
the requirements of the Uniform Plumbing Code
(UPC™) published by the International Association
of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials (IAPMO) as
to grade and connections to existing pipe and shall
also comply with this standard. This standard spec­
ifics requirements for the installation of the trench­
less insertion of polyethylene (PE) pipe for use in
sanitary and storm sewers.
