1324.5 Initial Pressure Tests - Medical Gas and Vac- «
uum Systems. Each section of the piping in medical gas
and vacuum systems shall be pressure tested. Initial pressure
tests shall be conducted as follows:
(I) After blowdown of the distribution piping.
(2) After installation of station outlet/inlet rough-in assem­
blies.
(3) Prior to the installation of components of the distribution
piping system that would be damaged by the test pres­
sure (e.g., pressure/vacuum alarm devices, pressure/vac-
uum indicators, line pressure relief valves). [NFPA
99:5,1.12.2.3.1,5.1.12.2.3.2]
