Step 4. Acceptance of the pump:
The flow coming out of the pump must be equal or greater than the flow coming into the sump:
(fixture units coming in)x(2 fixture units/gpm)<gpm pumped out
Step 5. Determine the number of fixture units discharging from the pump:
32 gpm x 2 fixture units/gpm=64 fixture units.
Step 6. Conclusions
Add the fixture units discharging from the pump to the fixture units in the horizontal drain and continue
checking sizing the system.
