Introduction
This manual provides office procedures for checking the design of sump pumps which
may be proposed in conjunction with grading plans and/or building site drainage plans.
Generally, these systems are to be maintained by homeowners associations, property
owners, or some other type of private maintenance.
General Information
A sump is defined as a low area which prevents the free passage of water, and results
in flooding of streets or private property.  Generally, gravity flow drainage facilities may
be installed for sumps; however, there are some drainage situations which would
require the use of a sump pump.
