1 Thermal efficiency (Et) is a minimum requirement, while standby loss (SL) is maximum Btu/h (kW) based on a 70°F (21°C) temperature difference between

ured volume in the tank in gallons.
2 ASHRAE 90.1 contains a complete specification, including the year version, of the referenced test procedure.

5 Electric water heaters with input rates less than 40 946 Btu/h (12 kW) shall be in accordance with these requirements where the water heater is designed to
heat water to temperatures of 180°F (82°C) or higher.
6 Refer to Section L 503.4.3 for additional requirements for gas storage and instantaneous water heaters and gas hot water supply boilers.
7 In the U.S., the efficiency requirements for water heaters or gas pool heaters in this category or subcategory are specified by the U.S. Department of Energy.
Those requirements and applicable test procedures are found in the Code of Federal Regulations 10 CFR Part 430.
